    How to play

    The moon drops a beam of light into the grid. Place pieces from the tray so the light reaches every ring — and passes through every star on the way.

      Tap a piece in the tray, then tap a cell to place it. Tap a mirror you placed to flip it, and again to take it back. Tap anything else you placed to pick it up. Tap any piece that came with the level to find out what it does — or tap one here for a demonstration.
