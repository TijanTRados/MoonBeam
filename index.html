How to play

The moon drops a beam of light into the grid. Place pieces from the tray so the light reaches every ring — and passes through every star on the way.

    Tap a piece in the tray, then tap a cell to place it. Tap a mirror you placed to flip it, and again to take it back. Tap anything else you placed to pick it up. Rings must be hit by exactly their colour — no more, no less.
